About DeepSync

We bridge research, policy and industry to turn innovation into European projects with real-world impact.

Our mission

Europe produces world-class research, but many promising technologies struggle to move from the lab to pilots, adoption and scale. DeepSync helps bridge this gap by connecting research organisations, industry, public authorities, investors and European funding.

Aligned with Europe’s priorities in competitiveness, digital sovereignty, clean industry, resilience, health and critical technologies.

Our services

From ideas to impact — across the European innovation ecosystem.

We help startups, research organisations, innovation hubs and industry partners access funding, build strategic partnerships and turn breakthrough technologies into real-world impact.

01 For startups, SMEs & research organisations

EU Funding & Consortium Access

We identify relevant European funding opportunities and connect your organisation with consortia, coordinators and strategic partners.

What you get

Funding roadmap, partner introductions and support to join European R&I projects.

02 For universities, innovation hubs & accelerators

Innovation Ecosystem Development

We help innovation organisations build international partnerships, connect their startups with industry and investors, and develop cross-border programmes and collaborative initiatives.

What you get

Strategic partnerships, internationalisation programmes, corporate connections and new collaboration opportunities.

03 For deeptech companies

Technology Commercialisation & Market Access

We connect emerging technologies with industrial partners, potential pilot customers and organisations that can support market entry.

What you get

Qualified industrial connections, pilot development opportunities and support in building commercial partnerships.

04 For EU projects & programmes

Programme & Project Implementation

We support the design, management and delivery of EU-funded innovation programmes, from coordination and reporting to exploitation, stakeholder engagement and ecosystem activities.

What you get

Reliable project delivery, coordinated partnerships, exploitation support and structured reporting.

Let’s build your next European partnership.

Whether you’re developing an EU-funded initiative, expanding your innovation ecosystem or bringing technology to market, we can help you move forward.

Selected success story

From consortium to funded programme

REWIRE

Funded programme · SPIN: Rise 2025

HealthTech & Wellbeing Commercialisation Lab

A programme helping DeepTech researchers turn HealthTech and wellbeing research into commercial opportunities through IP valorisation, entrepreneurship training and clinical validation.

Our contribution

DeepSync built the consortium and developed the EIT Digital SPIN: Rise funding application from the ground up. We also supported the programme with EU funding strategy, focusing on EIC and Horizon Europe opportunities.

The result
  • Over 20 scientists from EU academic institutions were selected with breakthrough ideas in Digital Health, MedTech and AI for Wellbeing.
  • More than 350 guests joined the networking evening in Lisbon.
  • 7 intensive weeks of bootcamps, coaching and hands-on commercialisation work.
